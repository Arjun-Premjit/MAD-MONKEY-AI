How It Works

Three steps. That's all.

1

Form your team

Find 1-3 friends from your school or coaching class who want to build something together. Tech background not required, just people motivated to solve a real problem.

2

Pick a real problem

Look around you. Something that bothers you at school, in your neighbourhood, or in your city. Tech or non-tech — both are equally valid. If it's real to you, it's real enough.

3

Submit three answers

What is the problem? Who has this problem? What is your solution idea? Answer those three questions and submit. Your name goes on the Mad Monkey Founder Wall permanently.
