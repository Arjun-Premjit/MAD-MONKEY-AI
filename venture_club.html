✦ THE 8 STAGES ✦

Every stage verified. Zero ambiguity.

STAGE 01

Stage 1 — Spark

Weeks 1–2

Idea posted, team forming. The founding Spark is submitted and a team of 1–10 is locked in.

STAGE 02

Stage 2 — Validate

Weeks 2–5

Proving the problem exists. Customer discovery: 10+ conversations documenting a real pain point with evidence.

STAGE 03

Stage 3 — Build

Weeks 3–12

MVP in development, first prototype live. A real person outside the team can interact with something.

STAGE 04

Stage 4 — Traction

Months 2–4

First real users, first revenue signals. Investor visibility unlocks here — founder-controlled, default off.

STAGE 05

Stage 5 — Revenue

Months 3–8

Consistent monthly revenue, PMF signals. Track A: in-app sales. Track B: paying B2B customers.

STAGE 06

Stage 6 — Raise

Months 4–8

Actively seeking investment. Direct investor introductions made by the Venture Club team.

STAGE 07

Stage 7 — Scale

Post-Funded

Funded. Hiring. Growing beyond founding city. Operational at meaningful scale.

STAGE 08

Stage 8 — Graduate

Months 18–36

Independent entity. You made it. Venture spins out as a fully independent company with full support.
